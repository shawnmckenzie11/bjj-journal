Submission defence
Open guard bottom, he starts a knee-cut: Frame, then Mobility, then Pyramid. Outcome: Guard recovered (knee shield / closed guard)
Mechanics
Inside forearm frames on his collar-side shoulder; knee shield across his hips
Hip escape away from the cutting knee to make room
Re-pummel the knee inside and re-square your hips
How each principle contributes

10 Frame: Bones, not biceps, hold his weight off your chest

30 Mobility: He's heavy, so you move: shrimp your hips out instead of pushing him

4 Pyramid: Plant foot + shoulder give a stable base to recover from

Common mistakes
Pushing with straight arms that get collapsed
Flat on your back with no hip escape
Frames on his head instead of shoulder/hip line
SMART target

When a passer cuts the knee, frame + shrimp before he settles.
Cue: Forearm on shoulder, then hips away · Situation: Positional rounds from open guard · Success: Re-guard at least twice in rolls

Coach examples

Sensei's examples for pre-class reading (not Gracie wording)

Closed guard: forearm across his collarbone stops him smashing down to your chest.

Mount: frame both hands on his hips before you bridge, so your arms aren't pushing on his chest.

Side control: forearm in his neck, other hand on his hip, makes space to shrimp.

Standing: hand on bicep or collar keeps him from crashing in on your legs.
